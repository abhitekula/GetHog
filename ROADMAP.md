# Roadmap

GetHog aims to be a calm, capable companion for the parts of PostHog that are
useful away from a desk. This is direction, not a delivery promise.

## Available now

- Saved dashboards and supported insight renderers.
- Event browsing with property inspection.
- People, sessions, session timelines, and replay when a recording is available.
- Feature-flag review and deliberate, confirmed changes.
- Demo mode backed by deterministic fictional data.

## Current limits

- GetHog does not render every PostHog insight or product surface.
- Replay depends on the recording format and may fall back gracefully when it
  cannot be displayed.
- A phone is best for reviewing and responding; complex analysis remains better
  suited to the PostHog web app.
- Network access, key scopes, and service limits can restrict what an account
  can show.

## Future directions

- Broader insight and dashboard coverage.
- Faster ways to triage events and sessions.
- Clearer offline and refresh behavior.
- Better accessibility, iPad workflows, and widget usefulness.
- More public demo scenarios and contribution-friendly test tooling.

Ideas and well-scoped proposals are welcome through the issue templates.
