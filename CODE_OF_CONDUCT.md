# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the GetHog community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Taking responsibility, apologizing to those affected by mistakes, and
  learning from the experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language or imagery,
personal attacks, publishing others' private information without permission,
and other conduct that is reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional
setting.

## Enforcement

The repository owner is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards and may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, commits,
code, issues, and other participation that does not align with them.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. All
reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly and promptly as circumstances
allow.
